About

I'm a science writer and emerging researcher whose work keeps returning to the same question: what is it like to be an animal? My writing covers animal cognition, interspecies communication, and the science — and philosophy — of non-human minds. My research background is in bioacoustics and spatial ecology.

The writing, research, and art are not separate projects. They are three ways of paying attention to the same thing.
