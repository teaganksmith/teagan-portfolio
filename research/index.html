Seconds-Scale Social Contagion in Nocturnal Flight Calls: A Calibrated Bound from Passive Acoustic Monitoring

A pre-registered point-process test for call-and-response behaviour in 35,402 expert Raven annotations from six autonomous sensors. The pre-registered test rejected its null in every sensor; a bandwidth diagnostic showed that rejection was a property of the null model. Two independent implementations were then adjudicated against series with known ground truth, and disagreed by more than the effect they were built to measure.

Python Raven Passive Acoustic Monitoring Point-Process Statistics

Categorical Structure in a Sperm Whale Coda Repertoire: A Replication with Transparent Methods

A replication of a 2024 Nature Communications result over Project CETI's 1,501-coda Dominica corpus, using deliberately simple methods. Of ten click-count classes, only one shows categorical structure that two independent tests agree on, and the most statistically striking result of the run was disqualified on inspection as three outlier files.

Sperm Whale Foraging Habitat and Deep-Sea Mining Acoustic Risk, North Atlantic

Species distribution modelling of sperm whale foraging habitat across the North Atlantic from 71,104 GBIF occurrence records, overlaid with ISA deep-sea mining exploration contracts and published acoustic impact zones. An adversarial verification pass overturned the project's original headline finding, which is documented rather than quietly corrected.

Methods & tools: I work with bioacoustic analysis software (Raven Lite, Audacity, R/seewave), QGIS for spatial analysis and mapping, and statistical analysis in R (ggplot2, tidyverse). I have experience with PAML for molecular evolution, phylogenetic reconstruction in MEGA and Mr. Bayes, sequence alignment in ClustalO, and tree visualization in FigTree. I work with NCBI and Ensembl for sequence acquisition and BLAST search, Python for large-scale biodiversity data analysis including multi-million record GBIF datasets, and Google Earth Engine for remote sensing and geospatial analysis. My field methods include boat-based transect surveys, photo identification, and systematic classification. I produce scientific illustrations as a working part of my research and write about science for public audiences.
