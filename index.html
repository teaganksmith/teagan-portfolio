Researcher · Science Communicator

Science that
listens to the
animal world.

I'm a science writer with two closely linked interests: the science of animal consciousness and language, and the conservation and policy implications of what that science reveals. From a background in comparative physiology and field research with whales, I write about what we're learning about non-human minds, and what we're obligated to do with that knowledge.
