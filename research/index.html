Emerging researcher in bioacoustics and spatial ecology. My scientific work informs everything I write.

Anthropogenic Disturbance to Beluga Whale Pods in Hudson Bay

Independent field research assessing the impact of human activity on endangered beluga whale behaviour during their annual migration to Hudson Bay, using boat-based transect surveys, systematic acoustic monitoring, and remote live-camera footage analysis. Conducted in collaboration with Beluga Bits, the Assiniboine Park Zoo, and Polar Bear International.

Global Coral Reef Health & Carbon Footprint of Marine Conservation Science

Spatial data analysis and visualization of global coral reef health indicators using MERMAID and R. Assessed geographic bias and carbon cost of marine conservation science, applying policy-relevant biogeographic analysis across global reef systems. Conducted at the Darling Lab, University of Toronto, under Dr. Emily Darling.

Evolutionary Genomics of C4 Photosynthesis & Cetacean Protein Evolution (NSERC-funded)

NSERC Undergraduate Research Award–funded position in the Sage Lab at the University of Toronto. Conducted evolutionary genomics research on C4 leaf structure assembly using PAML for molecular evolution analysis. Selected research paper: "Evidence of purifying selection in cytokine receptor 2 (CXCR2) reveals differences in cetacean rate of protein evolution" (EEB460).
