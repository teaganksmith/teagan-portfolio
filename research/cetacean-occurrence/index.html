Sperm Whale Foraging Habitat and Deep-Sea Mining Acoustic Risk, North Atlantic

Summer 2026

Project Overview

Spatial analysis modelling sperm whale (Physeter macrocephalus) habitat suitability across the North Atlantic (2000–2025) using 54,000+ OBIS occurrence records. Environmental predictors including GEBCO bathymetry, sea surface temperature, and distance to the Mid-Atlantic Ridge are used to fit a presence-background species distribution model. The habitat suitability surface is overlaid with ISA polymetallic sulphide exploration contract boundaries on the Mid-Atlantic Ridge and three published acoustic impact buffer zones (Williams et al. 2022, Science) to identify where deep-sea mining noise risk to foraging habitat is plausibly greatest. Outputs include a publication-quality overlay map and a reproducible GitHub workflow. Framed explicitly as a hypothesis-generating scoping analysis identifying priority zones for future site-specific sound propagation modelling.
