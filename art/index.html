Art

My art practice runs parallel to my scientific work, not beneath it. I make oil paintings and biological illustrations that grow out of the same questions my research asks — about ecological relationship, loss, and what it means to pay close attention to the living world at a moment when so much of it is disappearing.

The oil paintings are concerned with climate grief: the particular feeling of knowing the science and still having to find a way to live inside it. The biological illustrations bring the precision of field observation into a different register — form, texture, the logic of an organism's structure as a kind of argument.

Original works and prints are available in my Etsy shop. Process, studies, and new work on Instagram.
